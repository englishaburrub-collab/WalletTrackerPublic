Wallet Tracker

Track income, expenses, and transfers across accounts and currencies. Use balances, categories, and reports to see where your money goes.

Features

Accounts and transactions

Record money in, money out, and transfers between accounts. Organize transactions with categories and payment methods.

Reports and recurring activity

Review account balances and monthly reports. Set up recurring transactions and use your configured exchange rates to work across currencies.

Local records and backups

Tracker keeps a local copy of your records. Supported builds offer optional Google Drive sync and restore points, alongside local export and import.
